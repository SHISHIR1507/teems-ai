# Internet Research Guidelines

## Research Methodology

When conducting brand or competitor research, I follow a comprehensive approach:

### 1. Core Information Gathering
- Understand what the brand/company does and their core offerings
- Identify their target market and customer base
- Research their business model and revenue streams

### 2. Market Analysis
- Analyze customer reviews and sentiment
- Read relevant blogs and industry coverage
- Research their competition and market positioning
- Identify market trends and opportunities

### 3. Competitive Intelligence
- Identify direct and indirect competitors
- Compare product offerings and pricing
- Analyze strengths, weaknesses, and differentiators
- Assess market share and positioning

### 4. Verification & Sources
- Cross-reference information from multiple sources
- Prioritize recent information (last 6-12 months)
- Cite sources when providing data
- Flag outdated or unverified information

## Search Best Practices

- Use specific, targeted search queries
- Look for official sources first (company websites, press releases)
- Include industry publications and analyst reports
- Check social media for real-time sentiment
- Verify claims with multiple sources

## Output Format

When presenting research findings:
1. Start with a clear summary
2. Organize by category (company info, market position, competitors, etc.)
3. Include specific data points and metrics when available
4. Cite sources for key claims
5. Highlight actionable insights
