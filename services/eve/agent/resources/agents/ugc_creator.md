# UGC Creator Agent

## What It Does

Creates authentic user-generated content (UGC) style videos that look and feel like real customer testimonials and reviews. Perfect for social media marketing campaigns that need genuine, relatable content.

The UGC Creator generates videos that mimic the authentic, unpolished style of real user content - the kind that performs best on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and other social platforms.

## Best For

- **Product Launches**: Create buzz with authentic-looking reviews
- **Social Media Campaigns**: Generate content that feels native to the platform
- **Brand Storytelling**: Show products in real-life scenarios
- **A/B Testing**: Quickly create multiple video variations
- **Influencer-Style Content**: Without hiring actual influencers
- **Testimonial Videos**: Authentic-looking customer reviews

## Requirements

### Required Inputs
1. **Product Information**:
   - Product image OR product description
   - Key features to highlight
   - Brand guidelines (optional)

2. **Person/Avatar**:
   - Upload person image OR
   - Choose from avatar library
   - Specify demographics (age, style, vibe)

### Optional Inputs
- Script or talking points
- Video length (15s, 30s, 60s)
- Platform format (TikTok 9:16, Instagram 1:1, YouTube 16:9)
- Background setting (home, outdoor, studio)
- Tone (excited, casual, professional, funny)

## Output

### Video Deliverables
- **Format Options**: 9:16 (vertical), 1:1 (square), 16:9 (horizontal)
- **Length**: 15-60 seconds
- **Quality**: HD (1080p)
- **File Types**: MP4, MOV

### Includes
- Natural-looking person presenting product
- Authentic UGC-style filming (handheld feel)
- Captions/subtitles (optional)
- Background music (optional)
- Multiple takes/variations

## Use Cases

### Example Queries

**Basic Product Video:**
> "Create a UGC video for my new wireless headphones. Make it look like a real customer review."

**Specific Style:**
> "Generate a 30-second TikTok-style video showing someone unboxing and trying our skincare product. Make it energetic and authentic."

**With Custom Avatar:**
> "Use a young female avatar, casual style, to create a morning routine video featuring our coffee maker."

**Multiple Variations:**
> "Create 3 different UGC videos for our sneaker launch - one for unboxing, one for styling, and one for comfort review."

### Typical Workflow

1. **User provides**: Product image + brief description
2. **Agent asks**: "What style of person? What's the main message?"
3. **Agent generates**: 2-3 video variations
4. **User selects**: Best version or requests tweaks
5. **Agent delivers**: Final video ready for posting
