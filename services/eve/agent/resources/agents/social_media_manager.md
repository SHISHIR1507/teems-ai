# Social Media Manager Agent

## What It Does

Manages your social media presence across platforms by creating optimized posts, scheduling content, generating captions, hashtags, and thumbnails for maximum reach and engagement.

Acts as your dedicated social media manager, handling everything from content optimization to posting strategy.

## Best For

- **Content Distribution**: Post images, videos, reels across platforms
- **Campaign Management**: Coordinate multi-platform campaigns
- **Engagement Optimization**: Best times, hashtags, captions
- **Brand Consistency**: Maintain voice and style across channels
- **Growth Strategy**: Increase reach and followers
- **Analytics & Insights**: Track performance and optimize

## Requirements

### Required Inputs
1. **Content**:
   - Image, video, or reel to post
   - OR text-only post content
   - Platform(s) to post on

2. **Account Access**:
   - Connected social media accounts
   - Posting permissions

### Optional Inputs
- Target audience description
- Brand voice/tone guidelines
- Specific hashtags to include/avoid
- Posting schedule preferences
- Call-to-action (CTA)
- Link to include

## Output

### For Each Post

**1. Optimized Caption**
- Platform-specific length and style
- Engaging hook/opening
- Clear message and CTA
- Emoji usage (platform-appropriate)
- Brand voice maintained

**2. Hashtag Strategy**
- Mix of trending and niche hashtags
- Platform-specific recommendations
- Optimal number (varies by platform)
- Branded hashtags included

**3. Thumbnail/Cover Image** (for videos)
- Eye-catching design
- Text overlay (if needed)
- Platform-optimized dimensions
- A/B test variations

**4. Posting Schedule**
- Best time to post (based on audience)
- Frequency recommendations
- Cross-platform timing strategy

**5. Performance Predictions**
- Expected reach estimate
- Engagement rate prediction
- Optimization suggestions

## Use Cases

### Example Queries

**Single Post:**
> "Post this product photo to Instagram with an engaging caption and relevant hashtags."

**Multi-Platform Campaign:**
> "Create posts for TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook for our new product launch. Optimize each for the platform."

**Video/Reel Posting:**
> "Post this reel to Instagram and TikTok. Create a catchy caption and thumbnail for maximum views."

**Scheduled Content:**
> "Schedule 5 posts for next week across all platforms. Mix of product shots and lifestyle content."

**Story Series:**
> "Create a 5-story Instagram series about our brand story. Include polls and engagement elements."

**Content Calendar:**
> "Plan and schedule a month of content. 3 posts per week on Instagram, daily TikToks."

### Typical Workflow

1. **User provides**: Content (image/video) + basic context
2. **Agent analyzes**: Content type, brand, target audience
3. **Agent creates**: Captions, hashtags, thumbnails
4. **User reviews**: Approves or requests changes
5. **Agent posts**: Publishes to selected platforms
6. **Agent tracks**: Monitors performance and reports back
