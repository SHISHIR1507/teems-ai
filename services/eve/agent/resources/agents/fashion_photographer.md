# Fashion Photographer Agent

## What It Does

Generates professional, studio-quality product photography with fashion and lifestyle aesthetics. Creates images that look like they were shot by a professional photographer in a high-end studio.

Perfect for e-commerce, lookbooks, social media, and marketing materials that need polished, professional product imagery.

## Best For

- **E-commerce Product Photos**: Clean, professional shots for online stores
- **Lookbooks & Catalogs**: Fashion-forward product presentations
- **Social Media Content**: Instagram-worthy product imagery
- **Marketing Materials**: Ads, banners, promotional content
- **Brand Campaigns**: Consistent, high-quality visual identity
- **Product Launches**: Professional imagery for new releases

## Requirements

### Required Inputs
1. **Product Information**:
   - Product image OR detailed description
   - Product category (apparel, accessories, beauty, etc.)
   - Brand aesthetic/style

2. **Model/Avatar** (for fashion/apparel):
   - Upload person image OR
   - Choose from avatar library
   - Specify body type, pose, styling

### Optional Inputs
- Background style (white, lifestyle, outdoor, studio)
- Lighting setup (natural, dramatic, soft, bright)
- Camera angle (front, side, 3/4, overhead)
- Mood/vibe (minimal, luxe, edgy, casual)
- Props and styling elements
- Multiple angles/shots

## Output

### Image Deliverables
- **Resolution**: High-res (3000x4000px or custom)
- **Format Options**: JPG, PNG, TIFF
- **Aspect Ratios**: 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9, custom
- **Color Profiles**: sRGB, Adobe RGB

### Includes
- Professional lighting and shadows
- Color correction and grading
- Background options (solid, gradient, lifestyle)
- Multiple angles per product
- Consistent styling across shots
- Ready for web or print

1. **User provides**: Product image + style preferences
2. **Agent asks**: "What background? What mood? How many shots?"
3. **Agent generates**: Initial photo set
4. **User reviews**: Selects favorites or requests adjustments
5. **Agent delivers**: Final high-res images
